## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Sacred Direction is a devotional compass for Jain pilgrims, built around a single sacred place: Shatrunjaya. Wherever you are in the world, the app points you toward the tirth and shows the exact distance and direction — turning an everyday moment into a small act of devotion.

FIND YOUR DIRECTION
Open the app and the needle finds true direction to Shatrunjaya, updating live as you move or turn your device. See your distance to the tirth at a glance.

A MOMENT OF PRAYER
When the compass aligns with the sacred direction, the app gently reveals the Navkar Mantra with a soft haptic cue — a quiet invitation to pause and pray, right where you stand.

DAILY PRAYER REMINDERS
Set one or more daily reminders and the app will notify you at your chosen times, so a moment of devotion is never far from your day. Reminders run entirely on your device.

SIMPLE AND FOCUSED
- Light and dark themes
- Multiple languages
- No account, no ads, no tracking
- Works fully offline once your location is found

Sacred Direction was built for a single purpose: to help keep Shatrunjaya close, no matter the distance.
