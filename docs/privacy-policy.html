Privacy Policy

Sacred Direction · Last updated: August 12, 2026

Sacred Direction ("the app") is a compass app that points toward a fixed Jain pilgrimage site and offers optional daily prayer-time reminders. This policy explains what data the app uses and, just as importantly, what it does not do.

Location

The app requests your device's location (while the app is in use) solely to calculate the direction and distance from your current position to the sacred site. Your location:

  • Is processed entirely on your device
  • Is never transmitted to us or to any third party
  • Is never stored, logged, or saved anywhere

If you deny location access, the app's core compass feature will not function, but no data is collected either way.

Notifications

If you choose to set prayer-time reminders, the app schedules local notifications directly on your device using the operating system's notification scheduler. These reminders:

  • Are generated and delivered entirely on-device
  • Do not involve any push-notification server or external service
  • Are never sent anywhere off your device

Data we store

Your language preference, theme choice, prayer-reminder times, and which milestone banners you've already seen are saved locally on your device (using standard on-device app storage) so your preferences persist between sessions. This information never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Data we collect or share

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, analytics, or usage data. The app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, and it does not require or use an account, login, or internet-based backend of any kind.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a revised "Last updated" date.
